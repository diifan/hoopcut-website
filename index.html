A few quick answers about Hoopcut.

What can I do with Hoopcut?

Hoopcut helps you turn basketball footage into shareable highlights: automatically find and separate highlight clips, bring your moments together with AI editing, and add a Shot Tracer to show the ball’s flight.

How do I get started?

Search for Hoopcut on the App Store and download the app. Import a basketball video from your phone, find your highlight clips, create an AI edit or add a tracer, then export your video to share.

Can I try Hoopcut for free?

Yes. Search for Hoopcut on the App Store to download the app and try it for free.

What does automatic clipping do?

It helps find highlight moments in a longer basketball recording and separates them into clips, so you can spend less time searching through your footage.

How is AI editing different from clipping?

Clipping finds individual moments. AI editing brings those moments together into a highlight video you can export and share.

What is Shot Tracer?

Shot Tracer adds a visible trajectory to your basketball shot, helping viewers follow the ball’s flight in your video.
