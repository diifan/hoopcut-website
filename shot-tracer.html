What is a basketball shot tracer?

A basketball shot tracer adds a visible trail along the ball’s flight in a recorded video. It helps viewers follow the release and the path toward the hoop, especially when the ball is small on a phone screen. HoopCut uses the uploaded footage to create the effect. Review the preview before sharing: a trail is a visual editing effect, not a calibrated measurement of shooting mechanics or accuracy.

How to add a tracer to your basketball video

  1. Import your basketball video using the button above, or drag the file into the upload area. The tool accepts MP4, MOV and WebM files up to 512 MB; playback also depends on the video codec supported by your browser.
  2. For longer footage, choose a segment of up to 30 seconds. Include the release, the ball’s flight and the finish at the hoop. Keep some context before and after the shot.
  3. Let the tool process your clip, then replay the result. Preview Firework, Cosmic, Ice Beam and Sunset to see how the trail reads against your footage.
  4. Export the free Firework effect when you are happy with the preview. Other effects are available to unlock in the app. Watch the exported video before sharing it.

Choose footage that makes the ball easy to follow

Start with a steady camera angle that keeps both the basketball and hoop in view. Avoid zooming or panning during the shot. If another player blocks the ball, or the ball disappears into a dark background, try a clearer attempt or a shorter range. For pickup games, check that the visible trail follows the intended shot when several players move through the frame. Do not assume every recording will produce a usable trace.

Basketball shot tracer questions

Is this basketball shot tracer free?

The web tool offers free Firework exports for clips up to 30 seconds. Other effects can be previewed, with an app link to unlock them.

Can I upload a full game?

The web upload limit is 512 MB and the processed range is up to 30 seconds. Trim a short shot sequence for the web tool, or use the app for videos up to one hour.

Does a shot tracer count makes and misses?

A visible ball trail is not a shot chart or shooting percentage. Use a separate shot log when you want to record makes, misses and shot locations.

Why does the tracer miss part of the shot?

Motion blur, an obscured ball or a moving camera can make the flight difficult to follow. Try a clearer clip with the ball and hoop visible, then review the result.

Can I use this for golf videos?

This tool is for basketball footage. A golf shot has different framing and tracking requirements, so this page does not promise golf ball tracing.
