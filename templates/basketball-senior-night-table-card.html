STEP 01

Face the arrival route

Place the card where visitors see the name before reaching the items. Use the table-layout guide to leave space for memories and a clear action. Measure the actual location rather than treating the illustration as a scale plan.

STEP 02

Use verified identity

Match the name and number to the program. Keep the supporting line brief: it can invite a message or identify the team, but should not compete with the name.

STEP 03

Print as a flat card

Download the scalable SVG or PNG, check your desired physical size and choose a suitable holder. No fold marks or production bleed are included. Print one test before making the full roster.

Read the example

The fictional card invites visitors to leave a favorite team memory. Pair it with printed memory cards and a clearly labeled collection area.

One thing to check

Do not describe this file as a ready-cut folded tent. It is a flat 3:2 layout intended for a holder or another support you arrange.

Practical questions

Can it stand by itself?

The exported design is flat. Use a stand or separately planned support appropriate to your paper and location.

Can several cards share a table?

Yes. Keep the format and text size consistent and give each player comparable display space.

Are these additional Hoopcut app features?

These are free browser planning and design resources. Hoopcut’s app focuses on basketball auto clipping and Shot Tracer. The editor does not upload video or publish to a social account.
