STEP 01

Count recipients and items separately

A group of ten seniors may need twenty cards but only one shared display. Set quantity for each item rather than multiplying the entire event budget by the roster size.

STEP 02

Enter actual quotes

Use the unit price and separate extras such as setup or shipping. Enter tax as an extra line or extra cost when applicable. The calculator does not fetch prices or calculate tax rates.

STEP 03

Agree the total before collecting money

Check the combined amount with the organizer and record an owner. Replace assumptions before ordering. Keep optional individual gifts separate from a shared team commitment.

Read the example

The sample combines 20 cards at $0.50, 10 envelopes at $1.00 and one $8 display with $2 extra. The arithmetic total is $30.00; all amounts are fictional.

One thing to check

Do not treat the example dollar amounts as vendor prices. This tool performs arithmetic on your inputs and does not verify affordability, quotes or payment.

Practical questions

What formula does the calculator use?

Each line is quantity × unit cost + extra cost. The grand total adds the line totals. Blank numeric cells count as zero; invalid or negative inputs must be corrected.

Can I use another currency?

This version labels amounts USD and does not convert currencies. Use consistent units and relabel the downloaded sheet yourself if planning in another currency.

Are these additional Hoopcut app features?
