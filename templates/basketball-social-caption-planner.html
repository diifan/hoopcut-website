STEP 01

Describe the moment first

Write one factual sentence about the clip before choosing a joke or slogan. If the caption claims a winner, record or comeback, verify it against the actual game rather than inferring it from the selected shot.

STEP 02

Choose a fitting voice

Use short, funny or reflective wording to suit the player and moment. An inside joke should still make sense to the person featured. Borrow a starter from the caption library, then add a detail that belongs to this clip.

STEP 03

Check credit and approval

Note the people and material that need acknowledgement. Set a clear review status so a draft does not get mistaken for an approved post. This sheet does not publish or connect to a social account.

Read the example

The two fictional entries use different voices because a quiet practice moment and a team-created play tell different stories. Neither is a claim about an actual Hoopcut user.

One thing to check

Do not use a caption to invent the result of a game or attribute a teammate’s play to the wrong person.

Practical questions

Does this generate captions automatically?

No. It is an editable planning sheet. The linked caption library provides original starting ideas.

Can I use the same caption for every post?

You can, but a specific line is usually more useful to the audience. Match the text to what is visible in each clip.

Are these additional Hoopcut app features?

These are free browser planning and design resources. Hoopcut’s app focuses on basketball auto clipping and Shot Tracer. The editor does not upload video or publish to a social account.
