STEP 01

Begin with identity and a sound check

Record a short answer and replay it before continuing. Confirm the name and jersey number against the roster. The interviewer should know who will review the material and where it is intended to appear.

STEP 02

Ask for a specific example

A follow-up often produces a clearer answer than asking several new questions. Leave a pause and let the player respond in their own words. Do not write an answer for them and present it as spontaneous.

STEP 03

Keep a useful edit note

Note which response is usable and any sharing restriction. Keep the question with the source file so an editor understands the answer. An interview clip should not be edited into a different claim.

Read the example

The teamwork prompt asks for a practical example rather than a ranked judgment about teammates. The cards contain questions only; no response is claimed to come from a real athlete.

One thing to check

Do not use these prompts to invent quotes or to pressure someone into sharing a private story. A short voluntary answer is sufficient.

Practical questions

Can I print separate cards?

Yes. The print view presents each edited row as a prompt card. You can also download the data as CSV.

Does Hoopcut record or transcribe the interview?

This page is a planning tool. It does not record audio, transcribe answers or add an interview feature to the app.

Are these additional Hoopcut app features?

These are free browser planning and design resources. Hoopcut’s app focuses on basketball auto clipping and Shot Tracer. The editor does not upload video or publish to a social account.
