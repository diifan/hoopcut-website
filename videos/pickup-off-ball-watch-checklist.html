BEYOND THE REPLAY

Play notes by Hoopcut · Updated November 6, 2026

Choose one visible playerReconnect movement with the ballUse a compact observation logQuestions
01

Choose one visible player

After one normal-speed viewing, select a player who stays inside the frame during the approach to the shot. Follow that player alone on the next replay. Note their starting area and direction of movement. If they leave the frame or become hidden behind another person, mark the interval as unobserved instead of filling it in.

02

Reconnect movement with the ball

On a third replay, compare the chosen player’s movement with the visible ball handler. Ask whether the movement begins before or after the release. These are timing observations; they do not establish a called play or communication between teammates. The wide camera offers context, but the short edit omits earlier possessions.

03

Use a compact observation log

A useful entry has three fields: visible starting area, movement during the approach, and visibility at the shot. Keep one row per play. This makes two replays comparable without inventing distances or performance scores. Record the clip timestamp so another viewer can inspect the same moment.

Can I identify a designed cut from this clip alone?

You can describe movement, but the clip does not verify a play call or intent.
