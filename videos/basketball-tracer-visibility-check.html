01

Start with the basketball, not the effect

Play the recording once and ask where your attention goes. If the graphic is the only thing you notice, inspect whether it hides important action. Basketball tracer visibility should support the ball’s route. The player’s release and the rim still need to remain understandable in the same view.

02

Inspect contrast in the actual scene

An indoor basketball background contains changing light and texture as the ball moves across the picture. Watch the trail through that route instead of judging one attractive still. A line that is easy to find in one part of the frame may be less readable somewhere else. Record the specific area that needs another look.

03

Separate overlap from uncertain tracking

If another player hides the basketball, the picture may not show enough information to verify the path at that moment. Note that uncertainty rather than assuming a smooth line proves correct tracking. Also check for places where the line covers the ball or basket. These are different basketball tracer visibility problems and should be recorded separately.

04

Return to ordinary playback speed

A slow inspection can help you notice a problem, but a highlight is usually watched as moving footage. Replay your own finished basketball edit at the intended speed and size after checking the details. Consider how long the graphic remains visible before the next play. Record observations rather than inventing an accuracy percentage.

  1. Contrast

    Can I follow the trail across the whole background?

  2. Overlap

    Does the graphic cover the ball, hand or basket?

  3. Uncertainty

    Where does the recording hide the real ball?

  4. Pace and fade

    Does the trail read cleanly at normal speed?

Questions about this basketball clip

How do I tell whether the trail obscures the action?

Check whether it covers the ball, release hand or rim, then replay the whole sequence at normal speed.

Does a smooth line prove accurate tracking?

No. Compare the line with the visible ball and note uncertainty where the ball is hidden.

Can I restyle or retrack the trail here?

No. The player contains the finished edit and has no controls for changing its graphic.
