Play notes by Hoopcut · Updated November 6, 2026

Choose one continuous attemptUse three observation pointsKeep comparisons descriptiveQuestions
01

Choose one continuous attempt

Use a segment that shows the final dribble, the player bringing the ball into the shot and the release. Do not join a handling move from one attempt to a shot from another and call it a continuous transition. In a highlight collection, restart the observation at each edit boundary.

02

Use three observation points

Note the final visible bounce, the ball being brought under control and the release. Replay between these points at normal speed after any frame-by-frame inspection. This avoids overinterpreting a brief freeze frame that may look awkward even when the movement is continuous.

03

Keep comparisons descriptive

You can compare whether the ball remains visible and how the transition appears in each selected sequence. The clip does not establish which move is faster, more effective or easier to defend. Those claims need a defined test or game context beyond a short individual workout.
