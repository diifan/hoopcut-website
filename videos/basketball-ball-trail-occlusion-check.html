Inspect three important regions

Use the release, middle of flight and approach to the rim as separate inspection points. At each point, identify the real ball before examining the graphic. A bright line can blend with the ball or nearby lighting; visibility at one frame does not prove the entire track is clear.

02

Keep a specific correction list

For your own edit, write the time, affected object and proposed adjustment: reduce width, shorten persistence or change contrast. Replay at normal speed after each change. The embedded example is a finished video, so its trail cannot be switched off here. A fair comparison requires the matching original frames, not a different shot from the same creator.
