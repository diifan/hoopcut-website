01

Quote or describe the label accurately

When a label is readable, preserve its wording and make clear it appears in the source footage. If the text is partly obscured or too brief to read, do not reconstruct it from memory. A caption added to your own post should distinguish a source label from your own explanation.

02

Check whether the sequence supports the label

Ask whether the selected footage visibly demonstrates the labeled movement, or only shows a later portion of an exercise. A short excerpt may omit setup, repetitions, recovery, or coaching cues. Avoid turning an incomplete visual example into step-by-step instructions.

03

Give viewers a path to fuller context

When the full source is available and appropriate to share, link it and explain that the edit is selective. Otherwise describe only what the clip itself shows. This is especially helpful when viewers might mistake a highlight for a complete lesson or assume the creator endorses a paraphrased coaching claim.

No. Preserve the wording and avoid claims about outcomes that the clip does not demonstrate.
