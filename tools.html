GOOD TO KNOW

Before you make it yours.

Are the tools free to use?

Yes. You can use these browser tools and download their files without signing in. The Hoopcut iPhone app is a separate product.

What can I download?

The six design templates export SVG and PNG images. The nine planning tools offer CSV files and a print view. Each tool also includes an original example you can start from.

Will the tool save my changes?

Your edits stay in the current page. Download a copy before refreshing or leaving. These tools do not upload your entries or publish to a social account.

Do I need the app to use a template?

No. The templates work in your browser. Hoopcut’s auto clipping and Shot Tracer focus on the basketball footage you want to turn into highlights.
