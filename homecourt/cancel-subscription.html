Cancel a HomeCourt Subscription: Find the Billing Route

Start with the receipt. The place that billed you determines where to manage renewal.

Find your next step ↓

By Hoopcut · Reviewed Nov 11, 2026
Independent guide · Official references below

The quick answer

For an Apple-billed HomeCourt subscription, Apple’s instructions direct you to Settings, your name, then Subscriptions. HomeCourt says it cannot cancel App Store subscriptions on your behalf. A web purchase needs its own billing route; deleting the app is not a cancellation confirmation.

START WITH YOUR SITUATION

What do you need to do?

My receipt is from Apple

Manage the subscription through the Apple Account that purchased it. HomeCourt cannot cancel an App Store subscription for you.

Open Apple’s instructions ↗
I purchased on the website

Use your original receipt and HomeCourt support to identify the web billing route. Do not enter your payment information on this guide.

Open official HomeCourt support ↗
I cannot find the subscription

Check the receipt for the purchasing account and billing provider before attempting a different cancellation route.

Check Apple’s missing-subscription guidance ↗

These options guide your next check. They do not inspect your account or diagnose a recording automatically.

Identify the purchase account

Find the subscription receipt and check the billing provider and account. If HomeCourt is missing from the subscription list, first confirm that you are looking at the account used to purchase it. Keep billing identifiers private. This guide does not collect credentials, receipts or payment information.

Use the matching cancellation route

For Apple billing, follow the current official Apple instructions linked below and inspect the HomeCourt subscription entry. For a direct website purchase, use the original receipt and HomeCourt support to find the appropriate management route. Do not assume that leaving a team or signing out changes billing.

Keep evidence of the result

After completing the available cancellation action, record the displayed expiration or renewal status for your own reference. A cancellation, a refund request and account deletion are different actions. If the screen does not make the outcome clear, ask the billing provider rather than assuming that closing the app completed it.

Decide what records you want to keep

Before separately changing account access, inspect any workouts or videos you want to retain and use the available export options. Keep a playable local copy where you have permission to do so. If you only want a lightweight future practice log, a manual chart is an independent option; it does not transfer paid history or cancel an existing plan.
