HomeCourt Video Not Loading or Missing: What to Check

Keep the original session intact while you work out whether the video is unavailable, unsaved or simply not playing.

Find your next step ↓

By Hoopcut · Reviewed Nov 11, 2026
Independent guide · Official references below

The quick answer

A visible workout score does not establish that a playable video was saved. HomeCourt’s lost-video article says recovery is not possible when the video neither saved locally nor uploaded. That statement describes one failure case; it does not diagnose every loading error.

START WITH YOUR SITUATION

What do you need to do?

A local exported file plays

Keep that file. The remaining issue is accessing or sharing the in-app copy, not reconstructing the visible basketball action.

Plan the editing handoff ↗
Statistics exist but video will not play

Check connectivity and preserve the session. The score alone does not prove the video saved.

Open official support ↗
No playable copy can be found

Do not promise recovery. HomeCourt describes a case where unsaved and unuploaded videos cannot be recovered. Ask support about your specific session.

Read the official lost-video explanation ↗

These options guide your next check. They do not inspect your account or diagnose a recording automatically.

Locate every copy you actually have

Check whether there is a previously exported file in your own photo library or files. Try that copy directly and distinguish it from a thumbnail inside the app. Note whether the issue affects one session or all sessions. Do not erase app data or delete the workout as a first troubleshooting step when it may contain the only remaining copy.

Separate network-dependent playback from a local file

HomeCourt documents that viewing a video which is not cached locally requires connectivity. Retry on a working connection and note the exact screen or error. A file that plays locally but cannot be shared has a different problem from a session whose video never loads. Use those observations when asking for help.

Prepare a useful support report

Record the affected session date, device and app version, whether statistics still appear, and whether an exported copy exists. Keep the information concise enough that another person can reproduce the issue. Account-specific session links belong in official support correspondence, not in the public worksheet or a comment thread.

Protect the next recording with a playback check

After your next short session, confirm that the saved video opens before recording a full workout. Where export is available, retain a separate file and replay it outside the app. A second copy does not fix an already missing file, but it gives your future editing workflow a source you can inspect independently.
