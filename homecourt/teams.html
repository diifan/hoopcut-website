HomeCourt Teams: A Basketball Practice Setup Checklist

Get the first player working before inviting the whole roster. Make the recording and review routine clear.

Use the checklist ↓

By Hoopcut · Reviewed Nov 11, 2026
Independent guide · Official references below

The quick answer

HomeCourt documents creating teams, inviting players and reviewing team activity. Use the official instructions for account actions. The worksheet here helps a coach plan roles, a test session and a video-review handoff; it does not connect to or administer a HomeCourt team.

Write a simple first-session brief

Specify the activity, what the player should record, and what the coach will review. Include a short setup check so players know what belongs in frame. Avoid making the first session a long collection of unrelated drills. One clearly defined task is easier to verify and explain when someone needs help.

Separate access from responsibility

The published Team FAQ describes creating a team through Settings → Teams → the plus control, then inviting players. Check the corresponding controls in your version. Name the invitation owner and the person reviewing submissions. Have one player accept and test access before distributing the workflow to the roster. For younger players, involve the responsible adult in the sharing arrangement; an invitation does not establish permission to republish a recording.

Review one submission end to end

Have a player complete a short test, find the activity afterwards and confirm what the coach can see. Check video availability separately from the recorded result. If you plan to create a recap, confirm the export role and permission before collecting a week of footage. Record any additional plan requirements shown in the account.

Keep the recap distinct from the training record

Use the team’s practice records for review and a separate clip log for the moments selected for sharing. A highlight can celebrate effort without implying that every participant had the same result. Keep player identification and clip context accurate. The browser storyboard can organize that edit without taking over the team’s accounts.
