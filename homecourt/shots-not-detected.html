HomeCourt Not Tracking Shots? A Focused Check

Find out whether the problem is the attempt count, the result or the location. Then test one change at a time.

Find your next step ↓

By Hoopcut · Reviewed Nov 11, 2026
Independent guide · Official references below

The quick answer

HomeCourt’s support guidance emphasizes visibility, lighting and keeping the device still during recording. Begin with a short counted set rather than repeating a long workout. Use the result to decide whether to adjust the camera, correct an existing session or contact support.

START WITH YOUR SITUATION

What do you need to do?

Attempts are missing

Check whether the player, feet, ball and basket remain visible and whether the camera moved. Repeat a short manually counted set.

Check camera framing ↗
Makes or locations are wrong

Review the recorded attempt and correct only what you can verify. Do not invent a missing shot location.

Review correction options ↗
The video will not play

Treat this as a playback or saving problem first. A visible statistic is not proof that the associated video saved successfully.

Check missing video options ↗

These options guide your next check. They do not inspect your account or diagnose a recording automatically.

Name the error precisely

An attempt that is absent from the log is different from a recorded attempt marked with the wrong outcome. A location error is different again. Write down the expected attempts and makes before reviewing the app record. Avoid using only the best clips as your reference because those omit the very attempts you are investigating.

Use a small controlled check

Choose a spot where the player, ball and basket are visible. Record a short sequence while someone counts attempts manually. Keep the device in place during the sequence. Compare the complete record with that count. If you change lighting or framing, run a new sequence and label it separately rather than combining the results.

Correct the record carefully

The linked official support article describes session editing. Menu labels can differ from older FAQ instructions, so use the controls present in your installed version. Before making a correction, verify the result against the actual play. Correcting one record does not prove the same setup will detect the next workout accurately.

Send support something reproducible

If the issue persists, preserve the session and record the device, app version, approximate conditions and observed discrepancy. The official article asks for profile and activity links. Send account-specific information only through HomeCourt support, not a public comment. This page cannot inspect your account or repair its data.
