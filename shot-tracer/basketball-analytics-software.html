Define the question before choosing basketball analytics software

Start with the decision your data should support. You might want to review attempts from one location, find a particular possession or compare notes from two sessions. List the fields needed for that question, such as date, player, attempt result and source timestamp. Some products measure specific aspects of a shot: HomeCourt’s official analysis FAQ describes metrics such as release time and angle. Check the documented capabilities before choosing.

Keep your shot log connected to the source video

Give each attempt a stable identifier and use it in both the log and your clip filenames. Record makes and misses consistently, and preserve ambiguous attempts for later review. The basketball shot chart linked below is a separate browser tool for manual logging; use it when you want to record your own observations.

Compare like-for-like groups

Write down what a group includes before calculating a percentage. Ten attempts from one session and ten highlights selected from several sessions describe different samples. Keep the selection rule with the result so another reader can interpret it.

Keep the overlay separate from the measurement

Check counts, locations or timing against the footage and your chosen data tool. Save the tracer version as an edited copy for presentation, with any statistics taken from the record you actually verified.

Basketball analytics and video questions

Does HoopCut calculate basketball statistics on this page?

No. This tool adds a visual ball trail to a clip. Keep statistics in your chosen analytics product or manual shot log.

Can a bright trajectory show my exact release angle?

A displayed effect is not a calibrated angle measurement. Use documented measurement software when you need that metric.

What should I include in a simple attempt log?

Date, player, a consistent attempt identifier, result, source filename and timestamp give you a practical starting point for locating and checking the footage.

How do I connect a highlight to my notes?

Use the same attempt identifier in the source log, original clip and traced export. Add the effect name to the edited version.

What can I try for free here?

Upload your own footage, choose up to 30 seconds and export Firework. Review the result separately from any statistical assessment.
