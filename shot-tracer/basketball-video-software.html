Match basketball video software to your editing task

A single traced shot needs a clear source clip and an effect preview. A multi-play reel also needs a timeline or editor for arranging separate sequences. A coaching review may need notes and access to the original footage. Check that your chosen software supports the workflow you need.

Prepare a source clip that survives the handoff

Find the original recording and make a working copy. Keep the release and the ball reaching the basket inside the selection; avoid starting after the shot is already in the air. If you first trim on iPhone, Apple’s Photos guide explains how to save the trimmed selection as a separate clip.

Use filenames that describe the edit

For example, combine a date, a player identifier and the source timestamp. Add a short effect label to the exported filename so you can distinguish the traced version from the original without opening both files.

  1. Locate the original and note the start and end of your chosen play.
  2. Keep a playable source copy before trimming or adding effects.
  3. Check the exported file in the app where you intend to share it.

Preview a basketball tracer before the final export

Upload your saved clip and select a range of up to 30 seconds. Review the trail at the beginning, through the middle of the flight and near the hoop. If the effect follows the wrong ball or loses the flight behind another player, try a clearer section. Download Firework when you are happy with the preview.

Review the result at its viewing size

A clip that looks clear on a large screen may be harder to read in a phone feed. Play the exported file on the device you expect to use and make sure the shooter, ball and finish are still recognizable.

Basketball video editing questions

What part of basketball video editing does HoopCut handle here?

This web tool adds a shot tracer to a selected portion of your saved basketball video and lets you preview the result.

Can I combine several plays on this page?

The web workflow processes one selected range. Use a separate editor if your project needs a timeline containing several clips.

Which files can I try?

The upload accepts MP4, MOV and WebM, with a 512 MB maximum file size. Keep a copy of the source and confirm that its preview plays correctly.

Is the shot tracer export free?

Firework export is free for a selected clip up to 30 seconds. Cosmic, Ice Beam and Sunset are preview options here, with an app link for more effects.

Should I delete the original after editing?

Keeping the original lets you make a new selection, compare the effect with the visible ball and recover the surrounding play later.
