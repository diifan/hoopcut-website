Which Hot Shot basketball rules are you using?

Use your organizer's rules for the version you are playing. As a historical example, HomeCourt's NBA HotShot contest ended on January 9, 2020. It allowed one minute, one ball and no rebounder assistance. Made shots scored 3 points from either baseline location, 4 from either elbow and 5 from the top of the three-point arc. Players could attempt at most two layups, worth 2 points each when made. Making a shot from each of the five hot spots earned a 5-point bonus for each completed set. These values belong to that historical version.

Hot Shot basketball and tracer questions

How should I record a Hot Shot round?

Keep the shooting locations and basket in view, record the full round and save your timer and score notes alongside the original video.

Are all Hot Shot basketball games scored the same way?

Use the rules for the version you are playing. For an informal session, agree on locations, timing and scoring before recording.

Can HoopCut count points or validate my score?

No. The tool adds a visual trail to your footage. It does not score a round, verify eligibility or submit a competition entry.

Can I export an entire one-minute round here?

The web tool processes a selected range of up to 30 seconds. Keep the full round separately and choose a short excerpt for the tracer.

Which tracer effect is free?

Firework can be exported free on the web. You can preview Cosmic, Ice Beam and Sunset here, then open the app for more effects.
