For a Ferris State football fan who also plays basketball, this page edits a saved court clip. Bring your own recording; no team replays or football broadcasts are provided.

Keep the sport clear in your description

Describe the output as your basketball practice or pickup highlight. A fan reference should not make the recording appear to show a football game.

Select a visible shot for the basketball tracer

Keep the ball, release and hoop in frame. Choose a range of up to 30 seconds and mark the basket. Watch the preview through the end of the shot before saving the effect.

Check the trail against your recording

Look for parts where the ball blends into the background. Adjust the selected range or hoop position if the preview does not match the visible action.

Ferris State football fans and basketball Tracer questions

Can HoopCut add a tracer to football footage here?

No. This is the shared basketball Tracer workflow. The Ferris State football connection is a fan's own basketball session, not football tracking.

Does this page contain Ferris State football highlights?

No. The demonstration uses generic basketball footage. This page does not provide team games, player footage, schedules or results.

What basketball recording should I choose?

Use your own saved clip with a visible ball and hoop. Include the setup and finish so you can inspect the tracer in context.

What can I export without the app?

You can export the Firework effect free from the browser uploader. Other styles are previews here, with more effects available in HoopCut.
