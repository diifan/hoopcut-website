Keep the scope of each output explicit

A court-wide recording, an analysis report and a decorative excerpt contain different information. Label each file according to its actual purpose and keep a reference between them. If you need measured positions or team statistics, check the relevant system’s capabilities rather than interpreting a visible trail as that data.

Preserve the full view

Keep the wider recording when surrounding players or earlier possessions matter; a cropped shot excerpt can leave that information out.

Name the output honestly

Use labels such as source recording, analysis report and traced sharing copy so the files are not treated as interchangeable evidence.

Prepare a clear basketball shot for the tracer

Keep the basketball, shooting hand and target hoop in the frame. Choose a short passage that starts before the release and ends after the ball reaches the basket. A steady camera and a visible flight give you a clearer view of the added trail.

  1. Use the original saved recording when possible, before social-media compression.
  2. Select a passage of up to 30 seconds that includes the release and finish.
  3. Choose the correct hoop when prompted, especially when several baskets are visible.

Review the basketball trail before sharing

Play the preview through once at normal speed, then check the release, highest point and finish. The effect should follow the visible ball without hiding the action. Keep an unedited copy if you also want to review footwork, timing or the surrounding play.

  1. Check any moment when another player or the backboard blocks the ball.
  2. Export the free Firework effect once the preview is ready.
  3. Save the edited clip separately from the original recording.

Full court analytics: questions and answers

Does HoopCut provide full court analytics?

This workspace adds a trail to a selected shot video. It does not produce court-wide player tracking, tactical reports or automatic team statistics.

Can I infer player movement data from a traced shot?

The trail is a visual editing output for the ball. Use an appropriate verified analysis process when your task requires positions, movement or measured team data.

Which videos can I use?

Choose a saved MP4, MOV or WebM basketball video up to 512 MB. Select a segment of up to 30 seconds with the ball and hoop visible.

What can I export on this page?

Firework is the free web export. You can preview the other effect styles and follow the HoopCut app link for more effects.

Does the tracer measure shooting accuracy?

No. The tracer is a visual editing effect. It does not provide a shooting percentage, player rating or coaching assessment.
