Choose the kind of interactive basketball experience

Start by naming what changes in response to the player. A training activity may provide on-screen prompts or a record of performance; a friendly challenge may use rules agreed before play. HomeCourt describes interactive workouts and virtual competitions on its official site. The interaction on this page happens after recording: you select an excerpt, preview a ball-trail style and decide whether to save the edit.

Agree on the activity before recording

Write down the task, time limit and how participants will record the result. If you are using an app, follow its current activity instructions. For a session with friends, use the same rules for everyone and keep those notes with the full recording.

Record enough action to understand the result

Keep the start and finish of the activity in the original file, even if you plan to share only a few seconds. Check a test recording for a visible basketball and hoop. Avoid moving the camera during the shot you want to trace.

  1. Save the complete recording and any activity notes.
  2. Find one attempt with a clear release and visible finish.
  3. Keep the excerpt’s start time so you can return to the full sequence.

Explore the visual effect on one selected shot

Upload your saved video and choose a range of up to 30 seconds. Confirm the hoop if prompted, then inspect the tracer against the visible ball. You can compare Firework, Cosmic, Ice Beam and Sunset previews. Firework is free to export here. The edited copy makes a shot easier to follow visually, while the original retains the activity and its surrounding context.

Use the preview to make an editing choice

Replay the result and check whether the trail stays with the intended ball. Choose a different range or recording if the effect is interrupted by an obstruction. Changing the style is a visual decision; it does not change a recorded score or establish that an attempt met challenge rules.

Interactive basketball and video questions

Can I play an interactive basketball game on this page?

The interaction here is video editing: select an excerpt and preview tracer effects. It is not a live basketball game.

Does the tool connect to a training challenge?

Keep rules, scores and accounts in your activity service. Upload a saved video when you want an edited sharing copy.

What can I change in the preview?

Select the processed range and preview the listed effects. Follow the hoop-selection prompt when shown, then inspect the trail.

Can the exported clip prove a challenge result?

A short edit can omit attempts or the start. Keep the full recording and follow the organizer’s documentation requirements.

Is the interactive preview the same as a free export?

Firework is free to export. Cosmic, Ice Beam and Sunset are previews here, with an app link for more effects.
