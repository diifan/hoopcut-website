A published example: the NBA 2026 three-point contest

The official 2026 rules gave each shooter 70 seconds for 27 balls. Four racks held four one-point balls and a two-point money ball; the fifth held five two-point balls. Two additional blue balls were worth three points each. The possible total was 40. The three highest first-round scores advanced to the final. This is a dated NBA contest format, not a universal rule for every shooting challenge.

Write a complete rule sheet for your own shootout

Agree on your format before starting. Decide how many positions and attempts you will use, which balls are worth extra points, when the clock starts and what happens in a tie. Give everyone the same sheet and keep it beside the score record. Refer to the official event document when reproducing a particular competition.

Keep attempt order visible in the recording

Frame the shooting positions and retain the continuous round, even if you later share just one make. Number the attempts in your notes so a short export can be matched back to the correct place in the sequence.

  1. Record the event or house-rule version and its date.
  2. List timing, scoring, attempt order and tiebreak procedure.

Keep the round and highlight as separate files

An edited clip can draw attention to one ball flight, while the complete recording preserves the sequence. Give both files the same round identifier and add the attempt number to the highlight.

Three-point shootout and tracer questions

Does every 3-point shootout use NBA contest rules?

No. Check the organizer’s published format or agree on a written format with the people playing.

Are contest points the same as a normal game basket?

A shootout assigns points according to its own format. Use the playing rules for a regulation game and the event rules for a contest.

Where can I check the NBA example on this page?

The source below is the official 2026 State Farm 3-Point Contest rules PDF. Check a newer event’s document when following a different year.

Can HoopCut keep score or decide a disputed attempt?

No. The tool adds a visual shot trail. Retain your score sheet and complete original footage for any scoring review.

Can I export the complete shootout?

The web tool processes up to 30 seconds at a time. Keep the full round and choose an individual sequence for the tracer highlight.
