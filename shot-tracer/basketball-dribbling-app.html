Choose a basketball dribbling app for the activity

Decide whether you want guided ball-handling practice, an activity record or a video to review afterward. Check the training provider’s instructions for device placement and available feedback. HomeCourt’s official site describes ball-handling activities and camera setup. The HoopCut workspace below serves a different part of the session: making an edited video of a shot that follows your dribble move.

Plan a sequence the camera can keep in view

Before recording, mark where the move begins and where the shot finishes. Check that your feet, the ball and the basket stay visible through the approach. If you must choose between a close-up of the dribble and the full flight, keep the original wider recording.

Record the move, gather and shot as one sequence

Start recording before the player moves so the clip does not lose the approach. Keep the camera stable as the player gathers the ball and releases the shot. Avoid cutting at the exact moment of the gather: viewers need that transition to understand how the dribble led to the attempt.

  1. Play the recording once to check the approach and finish.
  2. Choose an excerpt that starts before the move and ends after the shot.
  3. Keep the original if your selected range leaves out earlier repetitions.

Add the shot trail without changing the practice record

Choose an attempt where the ball remains visible as it travels toward the hoop. The tracer’s purpose here is to highlight that flight, not to mark every dribble or score the move. Preview the selected range and inspect the trail near release and near the basket. Keep enough of the approach in the export for the edited finish to make sense to a viewer.

Share one example rather than a session claim

Label the clip with the move or activity you actually recorded. If you add notes about repetitions, keep those in your separate practice log. A short clip can show one sequence clearly without claiming to summarize all the work completed in the session.

Dribbling apps and move-to-shot video questions

Will this tool track every dribble?

The tool traces shot flight, not every dribble. Choose a sequence that ends with a visible basketball shot.

Can I upload a clip that contains only dribbling?

Keep dribbling-only recordings for your own review. Select a shot-containing clip when you want to use the tracer.

How much of the approach should I include?

Include enough approach to explain the move, plus the release and finish, within the selected range of up to 30 seconds.

Does HoopCut assess whether my dribble move is correct?

It adds a visual effect. Consult a coach or your chosen training service for an assessment of the activity.

Can I save a traced finish for free?

Firework exports are free for the selected web clip. Other styles are previews here, with an app link for more effects.
