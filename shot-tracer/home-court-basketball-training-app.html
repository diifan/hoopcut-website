Prepare your practice footage for a shot tracer

Choose a saved video with the shooter, basketball and hoop visible. A steady camera and a clear view above the rim help you follow the entire flight. Leave a little footage before the release and after the finish. Keep the original recording alongside your edited copy so you can compare the visible ball with the added trail.

Choose one complete shooting attempt

HomeCourt training and basketball video questions

Where can I find HomeCourt setup instructions?

Follow the official HomeCourt website linked below for its current camera setup, device and workout information.

Does this tool connect to HomeCourt or track workout results?

No. HoopCut works with a recording you upload and adds a visual trail. HomeCourt integration, shooting statistics and coaching assessments are outside this workflow.

What footage should I choose?

Start with a steady recording that shows the release, full ball flight and hoop. Include a short lead-in and the result of the shot.

What can I export for free?

You can select up to 30 seconds and export the Firework effect free. Cosmic, Ice Beam and Sunset are previews here; more effects are available through the app.

Why does a trail look incomplete?

A moving camera, motion blur or another player hiding the ball can interrupt the trace. Try a clearer attempt and compare the preview with the source.
