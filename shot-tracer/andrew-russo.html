Prepare your own basketball clip

Bring a local video file

Choose a saved recording you own or may edit. A search result or share URL is not a video file and does not grant access to the recording.

Choose the part of the basketball clip worth tracing

Keep one continuous attempt with the release, ball flight and rim visible. For a catch-and-shoot highlight, start before the pass arrives. For a pull-up, preserve the final dribble. End after the ball reaches the basket rather than cutting away during the flight.

Avoid tracing across a montage cut

A cut, sudden zoom or blocked ball can make the trail harder to review. Work on one clear shot before combining it with other plays in your usual editor.

Preview and export the traced basketball shot

Select your saved file in the uploader, choose up to 30 seconds and mark the hoop. Review the release and arrival at the rim. When the preview looks right, export the Firework effect free and keep the edited copy alongside the source.

Keep the effect separate from performance evidence

A tracer is a visual effect, not a shooting percentage, recruiting evaluation or measured ball trajectory. Preserve the original recording if a coach needs to review the unedited possession.

Andrew Russo: file and tracer questions

Can I paste a Hudl URL into HoopCut?

No. Select a video file saved on your device. HoopCut does not import Hudl URLs or access your account.

What if I cannot download the original clip?

Use the official Hudl help links and check the permissions available to your account. Ask the recording owner or coach for a copy you may edit. A share or embed link does not grant file access.

Is downloading from Hudl always free?

Do not assume so. Download availability and any cost depend on the Hudl product, account and video. The free Firework export offered here applies to the HoopCut effect, not Hudl downloads.

Will this tool upload the finished video to Hudl?

No. Save the traced file and manage any subsequent upload through your own authorized account.

Is the demonstration my team video?

No. It is a shared HoopCut example. Upload your own basketball clip to preview the effect on your footage.
