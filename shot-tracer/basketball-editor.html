Choose the basketball moment before adding effects

Start with a recording that shows one readable play. Leave enough time before the release to understand the setup and enough afterward to see the result. A very tight crop can hide the ball or basket.

Preserve the original recording

Keep the unedited file so you can return to the full sequence. Save your traced version with a different filename.

Check the shot tracer against the visible ball

Select a range of up to 30 seconds and mark the hoop in the uploader. Watch the preview through the complete flight, including moments when the ball crosses a busy background.

Review before downloading

If the trail looks wrong, adjust the selected clip or hoop position and preview again. Do not treat the effect as a shot measurement.

Keep your basketball edit focused on the play

Choose an effect that lets the action remain easy to follow. The browser workflow exports Firework free; the other styles can be previewed before continuing in the app.

Retain the surrounding context

Use an accurate filename or sharing caption. A tracer highlights the ball's movement without explaining the score or the full game situation.

Basketball editor questions

What does this basketball editor do?

It adds a visual shot tracer to your saved basketball recording. It does not generate new footage or assemble a multi-clip reel here.

How long can my selected highlight be?

Choose a range of up to 30 seconds. Include the release and finish so you can check the complete shot in the preview.

Which effect can I export free?

The shared browser uploader offers a free Firework export. Other styles are available to preview, with more effects accessible in the HoopCut app.

Can I keep a version without the tracer?

Yes. Retain your original file separately and save the downloaded edit alongside it with a clear, different name.
