  • Hot Shot basketball

    Record your Hot Shot basketball attempt, then make the ball's flight easier to follow with a shot tracer. Use the rules guide below to identify your challenge format and the HoopCut tool to create a short, traced version of a video you already have.

  • 3-point shootout rules

    Check the rules for the three-point shootout you are playing before recording. The NBA’s 2026 contest is one published format; your local event may use another. Keep the complete round for scoring, then use HoopCut to create a short tracer clip of one attempt.

  • Full-court shot points

    Under NBA rules, a legal made full-court field goal at the basket your team is attacking is worth three points. Extra distance does not add a bonus. Use HoopCut to add a tracer to your own recorded attempt while keeping the original.

  • Hot Shot game

    A Hot Shot basketball game needs an agreed format before anyone starts recording. Keep the rules and full round available, then select one attempt if you want a short visual highlight. HoopCut can trace that shot without replacing the record of the game.

  • Shot contest

    A shot contest works best when everyone knows the same rules before the recording starts. Keep the locations, timing and scoring written down. Then choose a visible attempt from your own video and add a tracer to create a short recap of that moment.

  • Hotshot game

    A hotshot basketball game can be a shooting challenge, but its rules depend on the organizer or version you are playing. Once you have your own recording, HoopCut can add a tracer to a selected attempt. It does not run the challenge, operate a timer, or calculate your score.

  • Hot Shot game

    Hot Shot game can refer to different games and shooting challenges. Identify the format you actually played before copying any rules. If your version produced a real basketball recording, you can use HoopCut to make a short traced excerpt from that footage.

  • Hot Shot challenge

    The final attempt in a basketball Hot Shot game can make a good highlight when viewers can follow the action. Keep the real challenge context, then add a tracer to the saved shot. HoopCut produces the effect clip; it does not certify a timed attempt or decide whether it counted.

  • Contest shot

    A contest shot may mean an attempt in a shooting contest or a basketball shot challenged by a defender. Either way, the surrounding action matters. HoopCut can add a shot trail to the saved footage, while the original clip carries the evidence about the play, participants, and outcome.

  • Hot Shots footage

    Hot Shots game basketball searches can involve a shooting challenge or a particular game. For a recorded on-court round, first identify the format and keep the scoring notes. Then use a clear attempt to create a short basketball clip with a visible shot trail.

  • Full-court shot points

    Under NBA rules, a successful legal field goal from beyond the three-point line is worth three points; a full-court release does not add a distance bonus. Check the rules for your own competition, then use your recording to prepare a separate traced shot clip.

  • Hot Shot highlight

    A basketball Hot Shot highlight works best when the viewer can see which attempt is being shown. Choose a clear moment from your own challenge recording and retain the actual context. HoopCut adds the shot trail; it does not establish the rules, award points, or turn an excerpt into a verified contest result.

  • Start a competition

    Start a shot competition by agreeing on the format before the first attempt: locations, time limit, scoring and how results will be recorded. Keep that complete record available. HoopCut can add a visible trail to one saved shot afterward, as a separate highlight of the activity.
